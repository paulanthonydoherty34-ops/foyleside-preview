Services & accessibility

Here to help

Level 3

Customer Service

Concierge, free Wi-Fi, lost property and gift card balance checks. Call 028 7137 7575 or email info@foyleside.co.uk.

Free to use

Shopmobility

Manual wheelchairs, rollators and mobility scooters, 9am to 5pm Monday to Friday. Registration required. Call 028 7136 8623.

Adult changing

Height adjustable changing bench, ceiling track hoist and space for carers. Ask any Customer Service Assistant.
